How to move around the site

The top menu appears on every page and shows which one you are on. Below it, a breadcrumb shows the path (Home and the current page).

With the keyboard
Press the Tab key to move through the links and Shift + Tab to go back. The first thing that appears is the “Skip to content” link, which takes you straight to the page text without going through the menu.
With the search box
Type a word in the box in the header and press Enter. You will see the pages and sections where it appears.
On mobile
The site adapts to the screen width; the menu is spread over several lines.

Frequently asked questions

How do I search for something on the site?

Use the “Search the site” box in the header. If it returns nothing, try a single word or check the site map on this page.

Are the videos and audio accessible?

The videos have subtitles and the Music audio states that it has no voice.

When was the site last updated?

The date of the last update appears at the bottom of every page.

How can I get in touch?

From the Contact page, with the form or by writing to the email address.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. The “Español” link in the footer or in the menu takes you to the Spanish version.
