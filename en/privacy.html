Privacy

What is measured on this site during the usability tests, with which tool, and how you can decide.

What is measured

Only during the usability tests of the User Experience course, and only if you accept, clicks, scrolling, mouse movement and an anonymous session recording are collected. It is used to draw heatmaps and see where people get stuck when using the site.

With which tool

The measurement is done by Microsoft Clarity, which stores the data on its servers and uses its own cookies. Clarity masks the text of form fields by default. This site does not ask for your name, email or any other personal data in order to measure anything.

Your choice

If you press “Reject”, Clarity is not loaded and nothing is measured. Your choice is saved in your browser and you can change it at any time with the button below.
