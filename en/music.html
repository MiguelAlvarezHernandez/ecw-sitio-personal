Music

Latin urban is the genre I listen to most: reggaeton, trap and R&B in Spanish.

My favourite artists

M

Mora

  • Puerto Rico
  • Reggaeton and trap

One of the most personal voices of the genre, with a melodic, dark sound that hooks you from the first listen.

Q

Quevedo

  • Canary Islands
  • Urban

The Canarian artist who conquered the world with his session with Bizarrap. A pride of urban music made in Spain.

B

Bad Bunny

  • Puerto Rico
  • Reggaeton and trap

One of the most listened-to artists on the planet, able to reinvent himself on every album.

J

Jay Wheeler

  • Puerto Rico
  • R&B and reggaeton

The romantic voice of urban music: heartbreak songs with an unmistakable style.
